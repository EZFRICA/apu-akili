Oversized central button with high-relief concentric rings and embossed microphone icon. Pressing it streams 16kHz PCM audio over WebSocket (/ws/eleven_english_sts_v2 or /ws/gemini-3.5-transcribe-live) for real-time speech-to-text, topical guardrail verification, and Socratic tutoring.
